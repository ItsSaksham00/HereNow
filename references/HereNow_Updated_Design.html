Typography and components

Find your next
“let’s go.”

Use the platform font: SF Pro on iOS and Roboto on Android. Titles: 28–32 px. Body: 15–16 px. Supporting text: 12–14 px.

  • 8 px spacing grid. Use 20 px screen padding and clear space between sections.
  • 14 px button corners; 20–24 px photo card corners. Keep touch targets at least 44 × 44 px.
  • Use icons with labels in navigation. Keep the main action easy to find.
  • Use dark text on lime. Reserve white text for dark green buttons.

UX decisions

Account access: the feed is available after sign-in.

Location: explain the request on first launch. Users can choose an area and radius without granting device location access.

Feed: nearest to farthest, with distance and post age visible.

Let’s Go: records interest and tells the creator. Confirmation explains that the creator was notified.

Posting: camera or gallery → manual details or optional AI autofill → review → publish.

Comments: each post has a public conversation visible to its viewers.

Notifications: request permission when useful, then keep updates available in the Activity inbox.

States to include in production: loading, no nearby posts, upload failure with retry, denied permissions, and expired activities.
